How to connect Derive (v2)
  1. Open derive.xyz, sign in and deposit USDC as collateral.
  2. Go to Developers. Copy the Derive wallet address and your subaccount ID into the fields above.
  3. Under Session keys, register this app's hot wallet address (shown top-left of the Wallet card) so it may sign orders.
  4. Click Verify connection.
How to connect Derive (v3)
  1. The hot wallet is your Derive account: leave the wallet address empty (or enter an owner wallet that registered this hot wallet as a session key).
  2. Fund the hot wallet with USDC and a little ETH for gas on the settlement chain (Sepolia on testnet; Ethereum on mainnet once v3 launches).
  3. Enter an RPC URL for that chain, save, start the engine, then use Deposit to Derive. The first deposit creates your subaccount; crediting takes a couple of minutes.
  4. Click Verify connection; the subaccount ID fills in automatically.
